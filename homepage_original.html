Atlanta Personal Injury and Malpractice Attorneys

Robert J. Fleming is an experienced Georgia trial lawyer whose hard work and determination have resulted in millions of dollars in recoveries for his clients. Mr. Fleming enjoys an "AV" rating in regard to his professional abilities and ethics. This is the highest rating given by the Martindale-Hubbell Legal Directory and it signifies that a lawyer has reached the height of excellence as a legal professional. This rating is the gold standard for attorneys and used to be derived from confidential peer evaluations by lawyers, judges and mediators throughout Atlanta, Georgia and the United States. Today, a 4.5 star rating or higher is required for an AV rating.

Mr. Fleming handles many different types of cases but has gained a national reputation for representing victims of dental malpractice and medical malpractice who have suffered serious injuries as a result of negligence. He has been regularly recognized as one of Georgia's "Super Lawyers" by Law & Politics Magazine and Atlanta Magazine. He has been admitted to practice in all Georgia and Texas State Courts, the Northern District of Georgia, and the Southern District of Texas federal courts*. In addition, he has successfully litigated dental malpractice, medical malpractice, personal injury and business litigation cases throughout the country pro hac vice (being admitted to appear in another state's courts for a specific case) with the assistance of local counsel. In this situation, Mr. Fleming would typically handle the technical aspects of the case and local counsel would be retained to ensure compliance with local rules and customs.

We have the financial resources and legal skills to successfully pursue even the largest and most complex cases in Georgia, with notable success in dental malpractice and medical malpractice cases.
